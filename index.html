Focusing on  

The field of innovation and smart applications is the path that combines creativity and problem-solving on one hand, with modern technologies and programming on the other, to build new solutions and applications or improve existing ones to make them faster, smarter, and easier to use.

Simply put:

It means not settling for just writing code or building a standard app, but thinking about how to make the application understand context, learn from data, save human effort, or introduce a new idea that wasn't offered in that way before.
